FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still stuck? Open a support ticket from your dashboard.

How do I start?

Register with your email and phone number, claim your ₦600 bonus, then fund your wallet and choose a plan starting from ₦3,000.

When are earnings paid?

Earnings from active plans are processed automatically once a day and credited to your wallet.

What does it cost to withdraw?

Withdrawals start from ₦600. A 15% fee is applied to each withdrawal to keep the platform running, and it’s shown before you confirm.

How do referrals work?

Share your personal link from the Referral page. When someone signs up with it and makes a deposit, you earn a commission in your wallet.

Is my money safe?

Your account is protected by secure sign-in, and every deposit, earning and withdrawal is recorded in your transaction history. Like any investment, returns carry risk, so only invest what you can afford.
